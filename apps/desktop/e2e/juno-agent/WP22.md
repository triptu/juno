## Crash points

All 16 names in `packages/agent/src/runtime/crash.ts` have scenarios:

- `inbox.committed`, `bridge.reset`, `bridge.submitted`: preserve both exchanges without duplicate bubbles.
- `approval.committed`: apply the durable approval once.
- `authorization.consumed`: show Failed and never send the consumed authorization.
- `attempt.beforeReceipt`: the fake receives one write; recovery never repeats it.
- `rotation.drained`, `rotation.committed`: keep a completed write, an open card and both exchanges across rotation.
- `redaction.deletionRaised`, `redaction.committed`, `redaction.purged`: distinguish an uncommitted removal from a durable removal and finish recovery.
- `backup.segmentWritten`, `backup.manifestRenamed`: keep the completed write and open card; finish a backup without another write.
- `ownership.released`: publish the move code after restarting and stay read-only.
- `restore.staged`, `restore.swapped`: restore the chat and open card, wait for review, then resume.

The runtime performs SIGKILL itself. `restartArmed` arms only the replacement utility process and removes the setting after the crash, preserving main's in-memory fake keychain. It does not change app code. Interrupted Move/Restore sheets are dismissed with Escape after recovery.

Rotation ages the persisted incarnation before the replacement process opens it. Schedule tests move the saved due time into the past while the app is closed. This avoids real-time waits, but is not an injected app-wide clock. The interrupted-state test removes the closed journal to exercise recovery from missing journal data.

## Remaining product and coverage gaps

Observed failures:

- Task Stop never reaches fake T3. The renderer's `cardCommand` requires an approval binding before handling Stop; Task cards have none.
- Ask again on an expired card after relaunch sends no new request. Expired cards likewise have no binding in the renderer's card map.

Inherited implementation gaps kept as concrete fixmes:

- Slack writes from the app's agent host are disabled, so the multi-send outcome cannot pass.
- Selected-span Remember and Remove are disabled in the message menu. Remembered/Undo is not wired. A removed optimistic message can reappear.
- Recall has no rendered recall citation. Its long-history specification is skipped and is not evidence of 5,001-message recall.
- T3 pending-question fake and adapter use incompatible shapes. Task Open/PR links, notification delivery, loopback preference, secret-blocked copy and the tainted `t3_send` Because line are incomplete.
- Schedule group labels, notification delivery and the message-menu reply-watch entry are incomplete.
- Offline chat currently starts a model request instead of holding it for the network.
- The provider settings offer OpenRouter and Custom endpoint, not Codex.

State coverage includes empty, idle, running, paused/waiting for Resume, AI off, too long, provider failure/retry, budget reached, offline chip, update needed, interrupted/retry, recovery/waiting for review, another device, and expired-card rendering. No passing app scenario yet proves not-delivered storage failure, blocked request sizing, budget exhaustion mid-reply or removal pending in a live job. Gallery fixtures were not rerun by this work.
