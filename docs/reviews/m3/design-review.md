# M3 design review: the sidebar and the empty workspace

Reviewed on 2026-10-13 at commit `86ee2ee` on branch `m3-review`. Scope: the signed-in shell, the sidebar (title bar icons, nav, quick filters, scope chip, topic list with Pinned and Recent, rows, empty states) and the empty workspace pane. Judged against `docs/design/handoff/SPEC.md` sections 2, 4, 5, 16, 17 and 18, `docs/design/inventory.md` section 2 and 3, `docs/design/friendly-layer.md`, `docs/design/tokens.md`, `docs/decision-log.md` and `docs/performance.md`.

The owner's decisions are taken as given and not relitigated: grey unread counts, icon-only Resolve in a reserved slot on the meta line, the hover-only scrollbar, Figtree with Coral and the rounder shapes, no layout shift ever, mouse-down activation.

Evidence lives next to this file: screenshots in `shots/`, machine-readable measurements in `harness/out/`, and the harness that produced them in `harness/` (section 4 says how to rerun it). Paths in the table are relative to the repo root.

Not verified: the app's own `juno.filter` measure (click to paint) against the 50 ms budget in `performance.md`; the scenario that reads it (`break pass: filter change timing on the large seed`) did not reach "Engine live" within four minutes on its run. The remount is the performance reviewer's call; it is flagged here because rapid filtering is the one interaction that broke.

  ```
  pnpm exec playwright test -c ../../docs/reviews/m3/harness/desktop.config.ts
  ```

  Eleven scenarios, one Electron app each: baseline light and dark with Coral, Claret and Cyan; keyboard only; window sizes and 200% zoom; reduced motion; empty states; the quiet workspace (fake clock 30 days back, everything marked read); pins and resolves through `user.db`; the hostile seed; the large seed; live message, socket drop, engine kill, engine failure; session expiry; and the scroll estimate. The harness writes `shots/*.png` and `harness/out/*.json` and asserts nothing itself, so a change in the app shows up as a changed measurement, not a failed test.
- Contrast: a tree walk over every text node under `aside` and the workspace, resolving each computed colour through a canvas (so `color-mix()` results are measured, not guessed), with the nearest painted background and the product of ancestor opacities folded in. The dimmed-row numbers in H4 were computed from the token hex values in `tokens.md` because the state does not occur in the fake's data.

## 5. Verdict

The sidebar holds its layout under everything thrown at it: no row overflows, nothing moves on hover, resize, live updates or an engine crash, and the hostile and large seeds render cleanly in both themes and three accents. The look matches the owner's decisions and the copy follows the friendly layer. It is not done. Five things are HIGH on sight and all five are small: Resolve is keyboard-reachable on one row only (H1), truncated titles have no full value (H2), the minimum window at 200% zoom cuts off the sidebar (H3), dimmed rows fail contrast (H4), and an engine that has stopped is invisible from the shell (H5). Fix those and the eight MEDIUMs in the three packages below and the sidebar is ready to carry M4.
