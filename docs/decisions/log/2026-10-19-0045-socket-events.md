- 10-19 00:45 · socket-events · Engine services hear socket events through `SocketEvents`, subscribed in their own layer, instead of a hook `SyncRunner` combines by hand. Each subscriber gets a bounded queue of 1024 drained in order by one fiber, after the event is stored; a full queue makes the sync wait with a warning rather than drop an event, since a lost notification is worse than a short stall. Subscribers restart for each sync in its scope. `onSelfPresence` stays on `runSync`, routed through `NotificationControl`: it is one boot value with one reader, not an event stream.
