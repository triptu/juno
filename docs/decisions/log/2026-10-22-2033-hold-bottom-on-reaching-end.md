- 10-22 20:33 · bottom-follow fix · A scroll of the user's that reaches the end (within 1 px) holds the bottom at once in the `scroll` handler, in topics and the Juno chat, instead of waiting for `scrollend`. With a scrollbar thumb held at the end, or fingers resting on the trackpad, a message that arrived before the scroll ended was not followed and the scroll then ended 53 px from the end, so later messages were missed too.
