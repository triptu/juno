The PDF frame needs no `plugins` preference. A voice clip plays `aac` and an audio file reads `duration_ms`, both assumed from the video fields, as no live clip was probed. Why: the owner asked to read common files without downloading them; Quick Look covers Office files and archives without Juno rendering them.
