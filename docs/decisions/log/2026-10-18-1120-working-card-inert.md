- 10-18 11:20 · working-card-inert · A working card's buttons are disabled, not only dimmed: ChatCard puts `role="group"` and `aria-disabled` around the footer buttons and the header Dismiss, and stops clicks there in the capture phase, which covers the mouse and Enter or Space, so each card's own buttons need no check. Focus stays on the pressed button. `inert` was not used because it would drop focus to the page and hide the buttons from screen readers instead of announcing them as disabled.
