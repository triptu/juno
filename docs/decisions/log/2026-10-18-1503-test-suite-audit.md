- 10-18 15:03 · test-suite-audit · Tests check what a user or caller sees, must be shown to fail by breaking the code they guard, wait for a condition rather than a time, and give every wait in a long walk its own bound; a desktop check joins the test whose journey reaches its state as a `test.step`, unless it needs a fresh app, its own launch options or a relaunch. Why: a desktop test pays about 1.9 s of launch, sign-in and sync before its first step, about half the suite's time; no test of an internal detail caught a bug in the week audited, while the e2e flows under load caught eight; and one unbounded wait in the gallery's shift walk cost every full run on main 13 minutes. Measurements and the list of what was removed are in `docs/testing-audit.md`; the rules in `docs/development.md`, "Rules for tests".
