- 10-17 20:41 · checks-after-send · Local fixes (typos, doubled words) show as soon as the send is accepted and never wait for grammar. Before, the post-send pass waited up to the one-second window for grammar, so with a live model the local finding reached the renderer after its one-second gate and was dropped. Doubled words now also count across a line break, and a doubled "is" is a slip unless it follows it, this, that, what or which.
