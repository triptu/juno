Until set, the notification hours are Slack's own schedule (its Do Not Disturb prefs, followed as they change in Slack, nothing paused when there is none), and Juno never switches presence by hours: Slack's automatic mode, with the tickles while the Mac is in use. Both used to default to weekdays 9 to 7, so a user who never opened Settings showed away in Slack every evening and weekend; one new user left over it. Hours saved before this that are still weekdays 9 to 7 read as not set, since that version saved them unasked; any other saved hours stay set, and those users get one `presence.set` active, unless a choice by hand holds, to undo an away Juno set.
