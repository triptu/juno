- 10-31 00:19 · Juno sets you away only when asked · A switch, Set me away outside active hours, in Settings › Notifications › Active in Slack decides whether the active hours set presence. It is off by default, also for settings saved before it (missing reads as off), so Juno never marks the owner away on its own. Off, presence works as with no active hours (Slack's automatic mode, a choice by hand kept by Slack), the rows are hidden and the hours kept. Turning it off after Juno set the owner away by the hours sends `presence.set` active once, unless a choice by hand holds; the first run with settings saved before the switch that hold active hours does the same. The flag is `awayOutsideHours` in the `notifications` pref beside `presenceHours`, and `awayHours` in `@juno/domain` gives the hours in effect to the presence job, the status line and the agent's `ownerActive`.
