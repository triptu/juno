- 10-18 13:10 · idle-cpu-every-process · Idle CPU has a budget per process, each of one core: main 2 %, window 2 %, graphics 2 %, engine 3 %, Juno agent 2 %, network 1 %, any other helper 1 %. `idle-cpu.spec.ts` holds every process to it in the states the owner leaves the app in (Home, a topic, the Juno panel and tab after a reply, Activity, five tabs, minimized, hidden, the first sync's card). A "Juno Helper" at 100 % on the owner's Mac was the engine's send workers waking at once while a channel's first unsent row waited out a backoff; the wake now comes from each channel's first row only (performance.md 1, 6, 8.12).
