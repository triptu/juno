- 10-27 13:43 · Follow new threads · A channel, DM or group DM can follow every new thread: a setting of Juno's own (`follow_new_threads`, with the time it was turned on), since Slack has none. While it is on, Juno follows in Slack, through `setSubscribed` on the background lane, each thread whose root is newer than that time when the root gets its first reply, live or found by the catch-up. Each thread gets one go (`auto_followed`, written in the follow's own transaction), and only a thread Slack has said nothing about is followed, so a Never is never overridden and a followed thread is left alone. No back-fill on turning it on, no unfollow on turning it off, nothing while the channel is ignored. Notifications are those of any followed thread. Modelled in `specs/follownew.qnt`.
