- 11-07 22:41 · r2-ui · A failed message picture keeps its glyph and its hidden `<img>` from the first error until a load: a retry in flight is the failed state with a try marked (`trying`), not a return to loading · The retry had cleared `failed` while its `<img>` was in flight, so the glyph blinked off for as long as the host took to answer (up to main's 15 s timeout) and a failed picture was never one stable state to look at or to test. Keeping the glyph makes a failure one state for the eye, lets a trigger during a try add nothing, and lets the gallery's settle check and the shift test read a failed picture without timing.
