- 10-20 16:26 · home-picture-cache · The engine's `PictureCache` downloads picture files into `<userData>/pictures/` (a `.part` file renamed when whole, two at a time) and main serves them as `juno://pictures/<name>`; when idle after Home paints, the renderer picks the next picture and saves it as next only once its small file is on disk, so a caught-up Home shows a picture only from disk and otherwise its scene. Why: Home's first frame never waits on the network and nothing arrives after it, the renderer never fetches, and the files stay for every workspace and across restarts.
