- 10-27 17:00 · Sidebar order takes own sends and live activity at once · Tushar: "sidebar update is taking time ... my own message send should immediately move that topic to top in sidebar, which doesn't happen." Two causes. A send wrote only its `pending_messages` row, so the topic's `topic_index` row (the sort key) moved only once Slack's answer or echo was stored; now `reindexTopics` counts a topic's pending posts as the user's messages at the time they were queued (activity, preview "You: …"; index version 8), the post writers re-index the topic, and the `topic_inputs` mark compares the pending posts, which live in user.db. And the sidebar kept its painted order through every sync cycle (boot, reconnect catch-up, the ten-minute sweep with its discovery search, the follow pass), then did not re-sort when the cycle ended, so a live message waited for a whole cycle and the next change after it; now a row with activity newer than the engine's start takes its place by recency at once, during any cycle, and only history the warm boot brings from before the launch keeps the painted order. Measured in `sidebar.flow.test.ts` on the laptop: an own send reaches the top in about 35 ms with Slack's answer held (before: only after the answer); a reply or DM during a catch-up in 31 to 37 ms (before: not until the cycle ended).
