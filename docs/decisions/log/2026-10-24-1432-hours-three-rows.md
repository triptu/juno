- 10-24 14:32 · Hours in three rows · Tushar: "let's just have three rows, weekdays(M-F), Sat, Sun", for both Notification hours and Active in Slack, to keep the UI minimal while covering most cases. Each section shows Weekdays, Saturday and Sunday, each with a from and to or Off; the day toggles, Different hours on some days and Same hours every day are gone. The hours stay per day underneath. Weekdays reads as the hours most weekdays have, so older hours with different weekdays load, and a change sets all five. One route, `notifications.setGroupHours`, sets a row of either set of hours and replaces `notifications.setHours`, `notifications.setDay` and `notifications.setDayHours`. The unused `DayToggles` primitive is removed.
