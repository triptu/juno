- 10-22 14:33 · agent · Every emoji and icon in the hover and selection toolbars is centred on the bar, checked from screenshots by `toolbar-align.spec.ts`. Emoji are set in `--font-emoji` in a 1em square moved up by `--emoji-14-shift`, not in Figtree's line box (1.5px high, 2px left before). Odd-size icons in even buttons draw in a box 1px larger (`Icon evenBox`), since the browser puts an SVG's box on whole pixels (half a pixel low and right before). `Floating` rounds its point to whole pixels.
