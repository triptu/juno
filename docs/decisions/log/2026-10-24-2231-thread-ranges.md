- 10-24 22:31 · thread-ranges · A thread trusts only the replies its pages proved, as Main trusts its synced ranges: every `conversations.replies` page records the range it covered in `thread_ranges` (replacing `thread_cursors`), two stored replies are continuous only when one range covers both, and the unread count is exact only when one range reaches from the read pointer to the end. `reply_count` proves nothing: replies arriving live raised it while a reply was missing, and after a deep jump the replies on either side of the hole looked continuous. Socket events never move a range; a page that reached the newest reply on an unbroken connection has a live tail, which a drop ends (held for followed threads, whose reconnect `getView` brings every unread reply). The topic view stops at the first unknown boundary and fetches across it as it scrolls there: down one page forward, up one step of at most two calls (a page from the range below, then a time bound at the density it measured). Existing threads start unknown and their first open fetches them. Modelled in `specs/threadranges.qnt`.
