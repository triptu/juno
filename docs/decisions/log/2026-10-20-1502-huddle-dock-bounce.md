- 10-20 15:02 · huddle-dock-bounce · Main bounces the Dock icon once (`app.dock.bounce('informational')`) each time it posts an incoming huddle's notification, also while the user is in another huddle, unless a Juno window has focus. A quiet card posts no notification and so never bounces. The bounce does not depend on macOS's notification permission and never touches the Dock badge, which stays the unread dot. Why: Tushar asked for one bounce when a huddle would ring or notify and Juno is behind; the notification is the one place main hears of it.
