- 10-24 11:31 · Tushar · The engine's outbox tests are four flow files, `outbox` (sends), `outbox-copies` (network copies and files), `outbox-actions` (edits, deletes, failures) and `outbox-reactions`, each under 400 lines, on one per-file helper (`outboxScenario`). They start warm on the synced workspace and read the message window and Slack's state, not internal tables; the exceptions are the outbox queue as the "action ended" barrier, the lookup's due time under a controlled clock and the queued state of a post sent back by a 429. The legacy-defect migration case and "refuses to edit someone else's message" are gone.
