- 10-20 16:25 · home-picture-hosting · Home's pictures are bundled as a catalog (`@juno/pictures`, its own chunk) with every story, source and licence, and only the image files live online, two WebP sizes per picture (640 by 432 for Home's box at 2x, under 60 KB; 2400 px for the picture page) in a dedicated public, read-only Cloudflare R2 bucket `juno-art` behind its r2.dev URL. Why: the stories ship and review with the code, the app never depends on a server for text, R2 serves public files with no egress fees, and a bucket of its own touches nothing else in the account.
