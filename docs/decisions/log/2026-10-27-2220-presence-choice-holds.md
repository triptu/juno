- 10-27 22:20 · A choice of presence by hand holds until the next switch, through a wake and a relaunch · Reported on 0.1.26: once the active hours had set presence, Set yourself away or active from the avatar menu did not last. The choice was kept only in memory in each sync session, so a wake (which applied the hours by force) or a new session put the hours back. It is now the `presenceHold` pref, with the time of the next switch and the hours it was made under: a wake, a new session or a relaunch tells Slack the choice again, until that switch, a later choice or a change to the active hours. It shows at once, before Slack answers, and a refusal puts the presence before back with a toast. A change made in Slack's own app holds the same way, as `docs/design/notifications/README.md` already said. Presence work has its own permit, so a choice no longer waits behind a notification's reading.
