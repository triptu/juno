- 10-24 14:12 · Hours per day · Tushar asked for different hours on weekdays and weekends, as Slack allows. Notification hours and active hours keep each day off or with its own from and to, as Slack's Custom schedule. The saved hours keep one set of days with a shared from and to and add `dayHours` for days that differ, so hours saved by older versions read unchanged and older versions read the shared hours. Slack gets `every_day` or `weekdays` when the hours fit them, else `custom` per day. Presence follows each day's active hours.
