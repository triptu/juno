- 10-17 15:20 · sidebar · The sidebar list holds its order while the pointer or keyboard focus is in it, as Mail does: contents update in place, moves, new rows and leaving rows wait and apply in one step 400 ms after both leave; your own action on a row applies to it at once, and opening a topic from elsewhere, Undo and Ignore channel apply everything. Keyboard focus means `:focus-visible`, so focus left by a mouse press does not hold the list after the pointer leaves.
