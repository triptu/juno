- 10-26 11:00 · The shift walk measures a state's touches with one layout-shift watch (`watchLayoutShift`): one observer, each check's last box snapshot the next one's first, one page call per touch that also waits for chunks, and anything moving between checks charged to the next · A profile on the runner found box snapshots under 1 ms; the cost was three page calls per touch and locator lookups. A forced full walk went from 3,847 to 1,872 job CPU-s and from 1,029 to 612 s on two cores. It still touches every sampled element six ways in every state. Skipping touches for elements without hover rules, or deduplicating alike elements, was not done: React and native listeners cannot be seen from the page, and identical DOM is not identical behaviour.
