- 10-27 20:17 · Draft sync simplified (Tushar) · After four review rounds that found bugs mostly in Juno deleting Slack drafts on its own and in drafts with files, Tushar chose a narrower design over another round of fixes. Juno deletes a draft only for a user's clear (the composer emptied, or Old drafts' Delete), with no exceptions: a save of Juno's that lands after another device cleared the draft stays in Slack. A send asks Slack to delete nothing. A post in the draft's own place names it and Slack retires it; the draft stays linked, its versions stored, until Juno sees Slack retire it, so if Slack refuses the post the draft, with any change another device made meanwhile, is the place's draft again. Deleting after a late save, and counting a sent draft gone at once, both lost newer text from another device in the fifth review. After a send into another place the old draft stays in Slack and Juno stops linking it. A text Juno sent from a place is never shown again, matched on the text the post carried, whatever the clocks say; a create unanswered when its text went is remembered by its `client_msg_id` until Slack shows it gone or changed. A draft with files from another device is shown but never updated or deleted, and typing there stays in Juno. The cleanup deletes, the `drafts.info` check before them and file-id tracking were removed.
