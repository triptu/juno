- 10-27 20:17 · Draft sync simplified (Tushar) · After four review rounds that found bugs mostly in Juno deleting Slack drafts on its own and in drafts with files, Tushar chose a narrower design over another round of fixes. Juno deletes a draft only for a user's clear (the composer emptied, Old drafts' Delete, or another device's emptied draft put back over Juno's own late save); a send asks Slack to delete nothing. A post in the draft's own place names it and Slack retires it; if Slack refuses the post the draft stays linked. After a send into another place the old draft stays in Slack and Juno stops linking it. A text Juno sent from a place is never shown again, matched on the text the post carried, whatever the clocks say; a create unanswered when its text went is remembered by its `client_msg_id` until Slack shows it gone or changed. A draft with files from another device is shown but never updated or deleted, and typing there stays in Juno. The cleanup deletes, the `drafts.info` check before them and file-id tracking were removed.
