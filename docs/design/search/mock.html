Look at: the chip carries the topic's art and title, and the In button shows it is set. One topic line, then every match in the thread, newest first; the topic line is not repeated. The reader stays on the same thread and steps between the four matches, landed on the second.

Typing an operator

"in:" opens a picker under the word being typed.

Look at: the picker's left edge sits under "in:", not at the field's edge. Rows are ⌘K's Conversations rows: 36px, the kind's icon or the person's photo, the matched part in semibold, the kind at the end. Enter or Tab turns the active row into an "in:" chip. The results behind keep their place.
