Search tab

Results on the left, the conversation on the right. Up and Down move through the results and the reader follows; Enter opens the topic, ⌘Enter opens it in a new tab and keeps you here.

Look at: the field holds a from: chip and plain words as one query, and the From button shows the same filter. Thread matches sit under one topic line (art, Juno title, place, newest time), with up to two match lines and "3 more in this thread". Main matches share one header, "procurement · Main". A topic whose Juno title matches shows with no message line. Rows below "Older matches on this Mac" are local only and move up as Slack's pages reach them. The selected match is the second line of the first thread; the reader shows that thread scrolled to it, every match marked, and the landing message in the brief flash that fades to nothing. Typing an operator

"in:" opens a picker under the field.

Look at: the rows are ⌘K's Conversations rows: 36px, the kind's icon or the person's photo, the matched part in semibold, the kind at the end. The first row is active; Enter or Tab turns it into an "in:" chip. The results behind keep their place.

Words to filters

Plain words search at once. The filters come after.

Look at: results for the plain words are already there. After a pause one quiet line offers the filters Juno read from the text; Tab applies them as chips. Nothing changes until you press it, and the line is gone when AI assist is off.

Answer from results

A question gets a button, not an answer card. The answer reads the matched threads and cites them.

Look at: a few sentences in the reader, each claim followed by a number. The cited rows carry the same number in the list; a number selects its row and shows it in the reader. Nothing above the results moves.

Beforethe empty reader offers the answer

Look at: when the query reads like a question and nothing is selected, the reader holds one secondary button. It never runs on its own.

Empty field

A new Search tab: recent searches and one line on operators.

Look at: recent searches keep their chips. The hovered row shows × to remove it. One muted line names the operators. The reader stays empty.

States

Each state says what happened. No spinners.

Look at: when Slack doesn't answer, the local rows stay and one line says so, with Try again. No matches names the words and offers Clear filters because a filter is set. While Slack is asked, local rows show and one muted line sits at the end, where Slack's rows will appear.

⌘K Messages

The palette keeps its short list and gains a way into the Search tab.

Look at: the last row, under a line, carries the text and filters to a Search tab. It is reachable with the arrows like any row, or ⌘⇧F from the field.
