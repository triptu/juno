Font, accent and shape options

Same screen in every panel (Juno friendly layer, signed in as Tushar). Reply with codes, for example "A2 + B3, rounder". Fonts are compared at the app's real sizes (12 to 20px). The accent is used on the primary button, selected nav, unread count tags, your own @mention chip, the focus ring on the composer, and text selection (the highlighted words in the last message).

AFonts with the current accent

A1 is today's Inter. A2 to A4 are free, SIL OFL 1.1 variable fonts, latin subset, 20 to 37 KB each as woff2.

Inter (current)Neutral and tall. Reads as a tool. The least personality of the four.
FigtreeGeometric with open, round letters and a friendly single-storey g. Made for product UI, clear at 12px. Runs slightly smaller than Inter at the same size.
Plus Jakarta SansThe most character: soft geometric, a little wider, warm numerals. Needs the most room, so sidebar rows truncate sooner.
DM SansLow-contrast geometric with round bowls. Calm and slightly playful. Looks lightest of the four at 12 to 13px.

BAccents with Inter

B1 is today's near-black. B2 to B4 add one warm colour with light and dark values. Look for: the Resolve button, selected nav and row, count tags, your @mention chip, send button, focus ring on the composer and selected text.

CCombinations light and dark, one with rounder shapes

C1 and C2 are one combination with today's radii. C3 and C4 are a second combination with the rounder shapes (rows 16, cards 20, pill composer and buttons, softer shadows). D1 shows the rounder shapes on the first combination so shape can be judged on its own against C1.
