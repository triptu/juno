Incoming huddle ring

When someone starts a huddle where you are, Juno rings until you join, dismiss it, or 30 seconds pass. These are three candidates. Ring plays one every 3.2 seconds for 30 seconds, as Juno would; press it again to stop. Juno plays them at half volume.

Invite

Two low notes rising a fifth. The join sound, an octave and more down and slower.

Rise Default

Three notes up a warm major chord, like a soft marimba. Plainly the join sound's family.

Swell

One low chord that swells in and fades. No rhythm at all, the calmest of the three.
